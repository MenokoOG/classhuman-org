05 —
Rationale

classHuman AI lives at the seam where human warmth meets machine discipline, so the identity pairs a grounded sunset-clay palette — pulled from the founders' earlier ventures — with circuit-trace geometry that stays precise, never cold. Every mark resolves a human gesture — a hand, a monogram, a decision node — into engineered traces that gate through one point: a literal picture of humans holding final authority over their agents. Warm primaries carry the LAHA spirit while a cool sage and disciplined type keep it tactical and confident — accessible AA-to-AAA in both light and dark.
